AI Mechanic — 1973 Mini Moke
Won't start. Single click when I turn the key.
TorqueIQ Single click points to a current problem, not a mechanical one. Three likely causes in order: weak battery, bad ground strap, or starter solenoid. Did you try jumping it?
Yeah tried jumping it. Still just one click.
TorqueIQ Battery's ruled out then. Where is the click coming from — engine bay near the firewall, or somewhere on the dash?
Engine bay, near the firewall.
TorqueIQ That's your starter solenoid. Here's how to confirm and replace it yourself:
  1. Bridge the solenoid terminals with a screwdriver — if it cranks, solenoid is confirmed
  2. Mini Spares UK part #GSO113 fits the Moke — ships to US in 5-7 days
  3. 30 min job, basic hand tools only
